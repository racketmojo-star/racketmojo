Every club has the same argument: who is the best padel player in the world? In 2026, the answer seems obvious to many — Agustín Tapia. But padel is a doubles sport, and that changes everything about how we answer the question.

## The 2026 Landscape: Tapia and Coello's Dominance

Agustín Tapia and Arturo Coello have been the world's #1 pair since 2025, and they entered 2026 as the reference point everyone measures themselves against. Tapia's left-side genius — his signature bandejas, impossible angles, and lethal touch at the net — pairs with Coello's raw power and towering smashes. Together they won the majority of Premier Padel titles in 2025, including multiple Grand Slams.

| Player | Role | Signature weapon |
|--------|------|------------------|
| Agustín Tapia | Left side | Touch, angles, bandeja |
| Arturo Coello | Right side | Power, smash, reach |

## Why the "Best Player" Question Is Tricky

Padel is almost always played in doubles, and the official world ranking ranks pairs, not individuals. So when we say "world #1," we're really talking about the #1 pair. That creates a genuine debate: is Tapia the best because he lifts his partner's level, or is Coello the best because his power dominates matches?

## The 2026 Contenders

Beyond the top pair, a handful of players could claim the title on any given week:

| Pair | Style | Case for "best" |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Tapia / Coello | Dominant, complete | World #1, most titles |
| Galán / Chingotto | Fast, aggressive | Multiple big titles |
| Lebrón / Di Nenno | Relentless, physical | Grand Slam wins |
| Navarro / Stupaczuk | Showmen, clutch | Consistent top-4 results |

Alejandro Galán is arguably the most complete right-side player of his generation. Federico Chingotto's speed and defensive genius make him the best defender in the world. Juan Lebrón has won more Grand Slams than anyone outside the top pair, while Martín Di Nenno brings unmatched consistency. Paquito Navarro remains the most electric crowd-pleaser in the sport, and Franco Stupaczuk's power keeps him in every conversation.

## The All-Time Argument: Belasteguín's 14 Years

No discussion of "best ever" is complete without Fernando Belasteguín, who held the #1 ranking for 14 consecutive years — a record that may never be broken. His argument is longevity: no one has dominated for longer. Tapia's argument is the ceiling: no one has played at a higher level than his 2024–2026 run.

| Criterion | The answer in 2026 |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Current form | Tapia / Coello |
| Peak level | Tapia's 2024–2026 run |
| Longevity | Belasteguín (14 years #1) |
| Big-match record | Coello's finals dominance |
| Pure talent | Tapia, by near-universal consensus |

The honest answer: Agustín Tapia is the best padel player in the world right now, with Arturo Coello the best right-side player and the sport's most feared smasher. But "best" depends on what you value — current form, peak level, or sustained greatness.

And that same logic applies to your equipment: the "best" racket isn't the one the pros endorse — it's the one that fits your game. Matching your racket to your level, your style, and your physical profile gives you a bigger advantage than buying whatever the world number one plays with. It's the kind of personal matchmaking that generic reviews simply can't provide.
