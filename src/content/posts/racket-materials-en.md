Every padel racket has two key materials: the **core** (inside) and the **surface** (outside). The surface is what touches the ball, and it plays a huge role in how the racket feels. Two materials dominate: carbon and fiberglass.

## Carbon: The Performance Choice

Carbon fiber surfaces are stiff, durable, and responsive. They give you crisp feedback on every shot — you know exactly where the ball hit the racket. Carbon also holds textured patterns longer, making it the preferred choice for players who use spin.

| | Carbon |
|---|--------|
| Feel | Crisp, direct |
| Durability | ★★★★★ |
| Spin | ★★★★★ |
| Forgiveness | ★★★ |
| Price | $$$ |

**Best for:** Intermediate to advanced players who want feedback, durability, and spin. Carbon rewards clean technique and punishes lazy hits more than fiberglass.

**Common carbon weaves:**
- **3K carbon** — Softer, more flexible. Better feel on touch shots.
- **12K carbon** — Stiffer, more powerful. Preferred by aggressive players.
- **18K carbon** — The stiffest option. Maximum power, minimum forgiveness.

---

## Fiberglass: The Comfort Choice

Fiberglass surfaces are softer and more flexible than carbon. They absorb more vibration, making them more comfortable — especially for beginners still developing technique. The trade-off is less durability and less spin potential.

| | Fiberglass |
|---|------------|
| Feel | Soft, dampened |
| Durability | ★★★ |
| Spin | ★★★ |
| Forgiveness | ★★★★★ |
| Price | $$ |

**Best for:** Beginners, casual players, and anyone prioritizing comfort over performance. Fiberglass rackets are also more affordable — most budget-friendly options use fiberglass surfaces.

---

## Mixed Surfaces: Best of Both?

Many rackets now use carbon frames with fiberglass faces, or carbon in the center with fiberglass on the edges. These hybrids aim to balance feel and forgiveness. The Adidas Metalbone CTRL uses a fiberglass body with carbon reinforcements — the flex of fiberglass where you need comfort, the stiffness of carbon where you need power.

---

## The Surface + Core Interaction

The surface doesn't work alone. Pair carbon with a soft core and you get controlled power — the surface delivers feedback while the core absorbs vibration. Pair fiberglass with a hard core and you get an unusual combination: a forgiving surface with a powerful core. These interactions are why two rackets with the same surface material can feel completely different.

---

## Which Should You Choose?

- **New to padel?** → Fiberglass. Softer, more forgiving, more affordable.
- **Playing regularly and want to improve?** → 3K carbon. The best balance of feel and forgiveness in carbon.
- **Aggressive player who smashes a lot?** → 12K or 18K carbon. Maximum power and spin.

But here's the thing: carbon vs fiberglass is just one variable. The shape, weight, and core all interact to produce the final feel. A carbon racket with a soft round shape plays nothing like a carbon racket with a hard diamond shape. Two "carbon" rackets can be worlds apart.

Matching the full package to your playing style is where the real value lies. Not just "carbon or fiberglass" — but the exact combination of all specs that fits your game.
