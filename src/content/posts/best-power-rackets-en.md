## What Makes a Power Racket Different?

Three things separate power rackets from control-oriented options:

1. **Shape** — Diamond moves the sweet spot high, maximizing leverage on overheads.
2. **Core hardness** — Firmer cores transfer more energy. Softer cores absorb it.
3. **Balance** — Head-heavy balance generates more momentum through the swing.

The trade-off is forgiveness. Miss the sweet spot on a diamond racket and you'll feel it — both in your hand and in where the ball lands. Power rackets demand consistent technique.

And consistency comes from having the right racket for YOUR specific game — not just the one with the best reviews. Your height, your swing speed, how often you play, even the surface you play on — they all matter. The perfect power racket for a 6'2" player isn't the same as for someone who's 5'8".
