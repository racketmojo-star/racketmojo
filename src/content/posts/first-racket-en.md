## Shape Matters

Padel rackets come in three main shapes: round, teardrop, and diamond. Each shape affects how the racket plays.

### Round
Round rackets have the **sweet spot centered** in the middle of the racket face. They're the most forgiving — perfect for beginners learning to hit consistently.

### Teardrop
Teardrop rackets shift the sweet spot slightly higher, giving you a blend of control and power. Great for intermediate players who want versatility.

### Diamond
Diamond-shaped rackets push the sweet spot to the top of the racket head. Maximum power, but harder to control. Best for advanced players.

## Weight

Most rackets weigh between **345g and 385g**. Lighter rackets are easier to maneuver; heavier ones deliver more power. If you're starting out, aim for 355-365g.

## Our Recommendation

If you're new to padel, start with a **round racket in the 360g range** with a soft or medium core. It'll be forgiving while you develop your technique.
